Omya provides innovative raw materials to the personal care industry

Our steadfast vision is based on three key components: a customer-focused culture, vast industry experience, and innovative strategies.

The first component – the foundation of Omya – is our customer-focused culture and our unwavering commitment to providing the highest quality customer support. Taking the time to understand each customer’s goals and product objectives, we believe this is truly what sets us apart.

The second component is our industry experience. Our team has been in the industry for decades and our understanding of the personal care market is vast. Supported by a dedicated leadership team, unparalleled technical expertise, experienced sales representatives and a passionate customer service team, we help customers create world-class formulations.

The final component is our commitment to providing state-of-the-art solutions. Our industry moves quickly. This prompts us to continually innovate, growing and refining the range of ingredients for our customers, always employing the latest technologies to deliver quality results. We’ve rounded up the best global partners, allowing our customers to formulate the best product necessary to excel in today’s competitive landscape.
