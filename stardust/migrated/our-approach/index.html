Dynamic & Customer-Focused

Our customers have always been our first priority. With decades of technical industry experience and specialty ingredient knowledge, we offer a wide variety of consultative support and educational resources to help our customers seamlessly through their product development and production process. Moreover, we realize that our success would not be possible without our customers success, which is why we’re so focused on building long-term partnerships. From raw materials to formulation support, we’ve got you covered.

Innovative Ingredients

We are a nationwide distributor of ingredient specialties for the personal care and cosmetic markets. As your trusted partner, we don’t just provide the raw material innovations that our customers need to excel, we provide the ongoing support that goes beyond ingredients.

Exceptional Customer Service

With over 20 passionate employees, we take the time to learn about your goals and objectives. As your highly-technical trusted advisor, we understand the complexities of the marketplace and will help you navigate through the development process. We’ll give you the support you need to stay on top of trends and ahead of the competition.
