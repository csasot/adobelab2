Omya Partners with Lipoid Kosmetik to Strengthen U.S. Presence in the Personal Care Market

Home / Insights / Blog / Omya Partners with Lipoid Kosmetik to Strengthen U.S. Presence in the Personal Care Market

October 29, 2025

Omya is proud to partner with Lipoid Kosmetik, a global leader in botanical actives, plant extracts, and phospholipid-based ingredients for the cosmetic industry. Through its U.S.-based subsidiary, Lipoid, Inc., Lipoid Kosmetik will collaborate closely with Omya to expand access to its high-performance ingredient portfolio across the United States, beginning January 1, 2026.

Backed by Omya’s strong technical expertise, market insight, and customer-focused approach, this collaboration will deliver innovative, natural, and science-backed solutions that address today’s evolving beauty and personal care trends. Together, the two companies will deliver innovative, natural, and science-backed solutions that meet the evolving demands of the beauty and personal care industry.

“We’re excited to work with Lipoid Kosmetik to bring their advanced, nature-inspired ingredients to more formulators and brands throughout the U.S.,” said Omya’s Sales Director, Anjali Martin. “Their proven innovation and our local technical support make for a powerful partnership that will inspire the next generation of beauty formulations.”

Omya looks forward to a successful collaboration that will strengthen Lipoid Kosmetik’s U.S. presence while providing customers with exceptional service, local expertise, and innovative ingredient solutions.
