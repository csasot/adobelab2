Introducing our newest supply partner, Quimivita

Home / Insights / Blog / Introducing our newest supply partner, Quimivita

January 31, 2023

Omya is pleased to announce our national partnership with innovative supplier, Quimivita.

Their natural and sustainable product offering is an exciting complement to our existing portfolio and provides a unique alignment with Omya’s focus in the Personal Care industry.

Quimivita has served the industry for more than 5 decades and their commitment to innovation is proven in their comprehensive product line that includes anti-aging, antioxidant, emollient, and exfoliant ingredients. Their environmental responsibility is shown in their upcycled, carbon negative, and vegan products.
