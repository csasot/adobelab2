Pacific Blends and Omya Specialty Materials Partnership

Home / Insights / Blog / Pacific Blends and Omya Specialty Materials Partnership

December 1, 2023

We are delighted to announce that Pacific Blends and Omya Specialty Materials have entered into a partnership in which Omya will serve as the exclusive agent for Pacific Blends in the United States.

Omya will represent the Vegelatum product line in the personal and home care segment.
