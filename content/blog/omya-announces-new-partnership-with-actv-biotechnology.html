Omya Announces Exclusive US Distribution Partnership with ACTV Biotechnology

Home / Insights / Blog / Omya Announces Exclusive US Distribution Partnership with ACTV Biotechnology

March 20, 2025

Omya is thrilled to announce a new partnership with ACTV Biotechnology, to distribute their innovative line of high-quality bio-active ingredients in the US.

This collaboration reflects Omya’s ongoing commitment to offering cutting-edge, sustainable solutions to the personal care industry, strengthening our position as a trusted provider of premium ingredients. By bringing ACTV’s advanced plant-based actives to the US market, we aim to support formulators in creating high-performance, eco-conscious products that meet the evolving demands of today’s consumers.

A biotechnology company that transforms the miraculous effects of plants into active ingredients

Founded in 2015 in Istanbul, Turkey, ACTV Biotechnology provides natural and effective solutions to the cosmetic industry, drawing on its deep expertise in plant biotechnology. These high-quality biotech active ingredients are derived from specially selected plants using ACTV’s patent-pending ProliCell® technology. This technology leverages plant cell cultures to cultivate rare botanical species without the need for soil, significantly reducing water consumption while eliminating challenges associated with seasonal, climate, and geographical constraints.

Through this strategic partnership, Omya and ACTV Biotechnology are committed to providing brands with cutting-edge ingredients with a strong focus on exceptional performance and sustainability.
