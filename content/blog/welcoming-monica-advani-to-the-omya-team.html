We Welcome Monica Advani to Omya as Personal Care Sales Representative for the Southeast Territory

Monica joins us with 15 years of R&D expertise and product development in hair and skin care formulations at companies such as L’Oreal and Crabtree & Evelyn as well as a contract manufacturer. In addition, Monica served as director of R&D at a startup manufacturer. In her free time, Monica loves to experiment with green ingredients in both the lab and the kitchen.
