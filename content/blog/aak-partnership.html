AAK Partnership

Omya is pleased to announce that effective January 1, 2024, we have partnered with AAK USA, Inc. to distribute their specialty Personal Care product line across the United States.

AAK is a world-leading supplier of sustainable, plant-based, high-performance specialty emollients for the beauty industry that offer unique features, advantages and benefits to meet new natural trends. AAK’s specialty Personal Care portfolio is well-known and represented by the Lipex®, Akocare®, and Akoline® brand names.
